A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Let’s start here.

Something else on your mind?
Sue is a conversation away.

Where can we meet?+

Call or email Sue to agree a time and a location that’s convenient for you. You can also ask about starting with a phone or video conversation.

What if a traditional bank loan doesn’t fit?+

There may be other lending options to explore. Sue can discuss your circumstances and the lenders and products she can advise on. Availability, costs and lending criteria vary; approval is never guaranteed.

What does advice cost?+

Sue is generally paid by lenders through commissions. Costs may apply, including for some non-bank lending applications. Read the current public disclosure statement (PDF) for fees, commissions, lender relationships and conflicts, and ask Sue what applies to your circumstances before proceeding.

Will I have to use open banking?+

The digital journey shown here is a proposal, not a live banking service. If a supported open banking option becomes available, Sue should explain the provider, consent, information requested and alternatives. No bank connection or financial information is collected by this demo.

Is the information here personal financial advice?+

No. This website concept provides general information. Advice for your circumstances requires a conversation and an assessment of your needs and goals. Ask Sue about the scope of her advice and her current disclosure information.
