03 / The words matter

Confident, with care.

The concept should sound human while keeping its promises accurate and supportable.

The relationship

“Sue works for you.
Banks work for banks.”

A proposed tagline from the brief. Sue should approve the comparison, and the live site must clearly explain her actual advice scope and make commissions and conflicts information easy to find.

Sue’s confirmation needed
More lending possibilities

“When a traditional bank loan doesn’t fit, explore other options.”

Use only with verified lender relationships and advice scope. Avoid implying access to every lender, the best rates or guaranteed approval. Lender criteria, terms and fees still apply.

Verify lender scope
Modern information sharing

“Less paperwork.
More progress.”

Where available, consent-based digital sharing could help provide the information a lender needs. The provider, bank coverage and privacy process must be confirmed before presenting open banking as an available SJA service.

Proposed future experience
i

A helpful process, without a promise of approval. Avoid absolute security or privacy claims, guaranteed turnaround times and promises of faster lending approval. Assessment and approval remain with the lender.
