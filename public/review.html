05 / The final content check

A clear review status.

Official FMA guidance reviewed — Sue’s launch sign-off pending

Current FMA financial advice and fair dealing pages were retrieved and read. The copy check considers the overall impression, support for claims, advice scope, commissions and access to clear public disclosures. SJA’s existing disclosure, privacy and complaints PDFs were also retrieved and reviewed.

The FMA explains that a website needs specified public information, with further disclosures at later advice and complaints stages. Disclosures should be clear, concise, effective, prominent and readable. Fine print does not automatically correct a misleading overall impression.

This is an editorial check against official guidance, not legal sign-off or FMA approval. Current register verification, a final full-regulations check, Sue’s approval and confirmation of any open banking tools remain outstanding.
